Amazon bridge

Reads amazon.ca buy boxes in this browser and forwards them to the monitor. The server never touches the site.

Use a Chrome profile that is not signed in to Amazon. Amazon scores sessions, and this is a store you buy from — a challenge earned here can follow the account, not just the address. Start at 1 concurrent read, watch the log for a blocked entry, and only raise it after a full clean day. A block pauses the bridge for an hour; do not shorten that.
