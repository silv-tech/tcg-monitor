Questions groups ask before they switch.

Who is Nocturne for?

Discord groups, card shops and resellers who want Canadian restock alerts in their own server. You run the community, we run the monitors.

What do I need to get started?

A Discord server where you have admin access. Join our Discord, open a ticket, and we set up the bot, channels and branding with you. Most servers are live within a day.

Do you monitor US retailers?

No, and we won't. Every monitor we run is a Canadian storefront, so every alert is something your members can buy and have shipped inside Canada.

Which games do you cover?

Pokémon, One Piece, Magic: The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Disney Lorcana, Dragon Ball Super, Star Wars: Unlimited, Riftbound, Flesh and Blood and Union Arena. If a retailer we cover stocks it sealed, we track it.

Can members set their own keyword pings?

Yes. Each member adds keywords with a command and gets a mention or DM when a matching product posts. You control which channels allow it.

Can I use my own branding and affiliate links?

Yes. Your name, logo, colour and footer go on every alert, and Amazon.ca Associates tags or other Canadian affiliate links are applied automatically.

How does billing work?

Monthly in Canadian dollars, charged at the start of each cycle. Cancel any time from Discord and you keep access to the end of the period. HST is added where it applies, and invoices are available for shops.

I'm not running a group. Can I still get alerts?

Nocturne sells to servers, not individuals. Ask in the Discord and we'll point you to communities that run Nocturne.

Missing a retailer? Tell us.

Retailer requests, feature ideas, partnerships, or anything else.
